# Database for Valuation of Unlisted Stock

**Source:** Japanese National Tax Agency (国税庁)

## Valuation Method: 類似業種比準価額 (Similar Industry Comparison Value)

The value of an unlisted stock is calculated using the following formula, based on the data from similar listed companies.

### Formula

```
Value = A * ( (B / B') + (C / C') + (D / D') ) / 3 * M
```

Where:
*   **A:** Stock price of the similar industry (`A(株価)`). See the selection rules below.
*   **B:** Dividend per share of the company being valued
*   **C:** Profit per share of the company being valued
*   **D:** Net asset value per share of the company being valued
*   **B':** Average dividend per share of the similar industry (`B(配当)`)
*   **C':** Average profit per share of the similar industry (`C(利益)`)
*   **D':** Average net asset value per share of the similar industry (`D(純資産)`)
*   **M:** Multiplier based on company size (0.7 for large, 0.6 for medium, 0.5 for small)

### Rule for Selecting the Stock Price (A)

According to the NTA's guidelines (section 182, "類似業種の株価"), you have the following options for determining the stock price (`A`) to use in the valuation:

1.  **The lowest of the last three months:** The lowest of the average monthly stock prices for the three months preceding the valuation date.
2.  **The previous year's average:** The average stock price for the entire previous year.
3.  **The average of the last two years:** The average stock price over the two years preceding the valuation date.
